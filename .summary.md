## Interpretation of core PLS

Core PLS is a strong, expensive scientific oracle and a viable domain paper,
but it is no longer being developed as the main ICLR novelty. Surface/GVP,
Physchem MoE, hard-pair ranking, and latent endpoint candidates contribute small
ensemble diversity rather than a dominant architecture breakthrough.

The latent endpoint evidence supports the name “shared latent + endpoint-specific
residual model”; do not call the learned scalar identifiable intrinsic
solubility without a future identifiability constraint and assay metadata.

## EditFlow / Path-OLD

The active methods direction is budgeted, path-aware distillation of an expensive
scientific oracle over a discrete edit graph. The target claim is not that edge
loss is new; it is path-aware, query-efficient oracle distillation with explicit
acquisition/generalization separation.

Permanent evaluation rules:

- report acquisition regret, held-out novel-design regret, and campaign regret
  separately;
- objective comparisons use identical queried node IDs;
- acquisition comparisons use identical initial sets, candidate universes,
  unique-node budgets, and cost models, while acquired IDs may differ;
- GB1 has become a development landscape and is not a blind test;
- FLIP2/TrpB test rows have not been downloaded or inspected.

GB1 random, greedy, UCB, Thompson, and occupancy-only development baselines have
completed on GPU 6 and are consolidated in
`analysis/editflow_gb1_standard_acquisition_development_matrix_v1.md`. Their
radius-2 normalized novel-design regret AUCs are 2.8035 (random), 2.0757
(greedy), 1.9502 (UCB), 2.0416 (Thompson), and 1.8662 (occupancy). Occupancy is
strongest locally while UCB is more competitive at radius 4; this is development
evidence for an exploitation/coverage trade-off, not a positive adaptive-method
result. The hybrid and uncertainty v2 final-checkpoint regret audits
completed; the path audit stopped after anchor 9 and must be rerun if that
comparison is needed. Do not turn post-hoc cells into confirmatory claims.

Adaptive development code now records normalized path entropy, effective path
support, and ensemble endpoint consensus, and deterministically allocates the
remaining budget to UCB exploration. The conformal edge-error utility requires
held-out or cross-fitted residuals by contract; raw ensemble uncertainty is not
claimed to upper-bound edit-field error.
